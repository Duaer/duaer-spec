# Source (provenance)

**duaer-spec** is an independent methodology repository:
https://github.com/fujiezee/duaer-spec

Early drafts borrowed ideas from Spec-Driven Development practice and from
product agent-ops rules. Those sources are **not** runtime dependencies. The
method shipped here (`.duaer/`, `duaer-*` skills, `DUADER.md`, `AGENTS.md`) is
owned and evolved in this repository.

## What we keep evolving here

| Piece | Role |
|---|---|
| `AGENTS.md` + `docs/agent/` | Agent ops (isolation, commits, Issue/PR) |
| `.duaer/` + `DUADER.md` + `.cursor/skills/duaer-*` | Duaer Spec-Driven method |
| `examples/` | Optional product overlays |

## Precedence

`AGENTS.md` / `docs/agent/` > Duaer method > `examples/`.
