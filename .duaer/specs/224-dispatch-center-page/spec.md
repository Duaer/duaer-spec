# 224 — Dispatch center is its own page

## Goal

Dispatch center opens a separate page, not a panel over the desk.

## Why

The first version hid the three-column desk in place. It needs its own page.

## Acceptance

1. The top Dispatch center button and the dispatch-card button each open `/dispatch-center.html` in a new page.
2. That page has a title, a 100px project rail, and the selected project's dispatch graph.
3. The desk itself no longer hides behind an in-page overlay.
